How to play

  1. Choose a case. Watch the opening scene or skip to the board. You can reread the story under “Read the case briefing.”
  2. Investigate. Pick up any evidence card in any order, then choose “Put down” to return to the board. Opening cards doesn’t cost a guess.
  3. Use hints if you get stuck. Some cards have a “Show hint” button. A hint points out what to look at and counts separately in your report.
  4. Keep case notes. Save a short observation from an open card. Reopen your notebook from the corner to review your notes. Taking notes is optional.
  5. Compare evidence. After opening two different cards, reopen either one and choose “Compare cards” in the corner. Pick another card you’ve already read.
  6. File your theory. Type one item or short phrase. You can also choose an evidence card that shaped your theory. You can guess whenever you’re ready, and you have three attempts total. After a wrong guess you can keep investigating.
  7. Read the report. A correct guess or three wrong guesses ends the case. The report shows the answer, your stats, and how the evidence fits. Find it again under “Previous cases.”
